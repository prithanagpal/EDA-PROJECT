Vellore Ecological Diagnostic Commentary

Vellore's ecosystem is heavily governed by the Northeast Monsoon (October to December), which contributes over 50% of the district's annual precipitation. During the pre-monsoon dry season (March to May), daytime temperatures frequently surpass 40°C, and the sandy riverbed of the Palar loses surface moisture. The PyTorch LSTM Autoencoder isolates early warning signals of critical slowing down by analyzing the recovery rate after these high-temperature summer stress cycles.

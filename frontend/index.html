The Problem

Ecosystems don't just collapse overnight. Long before desertification or deforestation becomes visible to the naked eye, the ecosystem begins to lose its resilience.

Most monitoring systems wait until the damage is done. We need a way to detect the subtle, non-linear signals of stress hidden within the data.

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)

NDVI Time-Series

We use satellite imagery to monitor the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) over time. This gives us a quantitative measure of "greenness" and vegetation health.

But simple thresholding isn't enough to capture complex seasonal dynamics.

The LSTM Autoencoder

1. Encode

Compress historical NDVI sequences into a dense latent representation.

2. Decode

Attempt to perfectly reconstruct the sequence based solely on healthy historical patterns.

3. Detect

High reconstruction error means the ecosystem is behaving irregularly.
